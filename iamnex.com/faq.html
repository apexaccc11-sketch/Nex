Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how NEX — your 24/7 AI Receptionist — replaces missed calls with consistency, speed, and professional customer care.

What exactly is an AI Receptionist?

An AI receptionist answers calls, schedules appointments, handles FAQs, sends follow-ups, and works 24/7 with perfect consistency. It functions just like a trained office receptionist — without missed calls or human fatigue.

How fast can NEX be set up?

Most businesses are fully configured within 24–48 hours. We customize workflows, import FAQs, sync your calendar, and launch quickly.

Does NEX sound human or robotic?

NEX uses natural, human-like conversational AI — far from the robotic systems of the past. Tone, pauses, and personality are customizable.

Can the AI handle after-hours calls?

Yes — NEX operates 24/7/365, ensuring every caller gets immediate assistance regardless of the time.

What if the AI cannot answer a question?

NEX escalates the call to your team or logs it as a priority callback. You never lose a lead — even when the question is complex.

Does NEX integrate with my existing systems?

Yes — calendars, booking systems, CRMs, and workflow tools. NEX can create appointments, leave notes, and trigger automations.

Is my data secure?

NEX uses enterprise-grade encryption, secure cloud infrastructure, and strict confidentiality protocols.

Can the AI speak multiple languages?

Yes — NEX supports 30+ languages and accents, making it ideal for diverse communities or multilingual service areas.
