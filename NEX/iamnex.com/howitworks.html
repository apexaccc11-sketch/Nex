How Your Automated Receptionist Works

NEX answers every call, in over 30 languages, books appointments directly into your calendar, and sends automatic reminders — so your business runs smoothly even when you’re busy or closed.

1. Always-On Answering — On Your Terms

Simply forward calls from your existing business line to your NEX number. From that moment, NEX answers every call professionally — 24/7 — or only when you choose.

  • Forward all calls, after-hours only, or missed calls only.
  • Easily turn forwarding off anytime to answer calls yourself.
  • No provider changes — your current number stays the same.

Forward ON → NEX answers instantly. Forward OFF → your phone rings as usual. You stay fully in control.

2. Speaks 30+ Languages Automatically

NEX detects or uses the caller’s preferred language and handles the full conversation naturally — both over the phone and through SMS.

  • Supports 30+ languages instantly.
  • Perfect for diverse communities and high-traffic locations.
  • Helps every customer feel understood and welcomed.

Your business becomes instantly multilingual — without hiring additional staff.

3. Direct Calendar Integration & Smart Scheduling

NEX connects directly to your calendar via the app so it can instantly see your availability and book appointments accurately — without double-booking.

  • Reads your real-time availability every second.
  • Books, reschedules, or cancels instantly during the call or SMS chat.
  • Follows your business rules for appointment length, buffer time, etc.

Every booking appears instantly inside your calendar — no manual work required.

4. Automatic SMS Reminders That Reduce No-Shows

NEX automatically sends warm reminder texts before every appointment to tighten your schedule and prevent missed visits.

  • SMS reminders sent days + hours before appointments.
  • Customers can confirm, cancel, or reschedule instantly.
  • Cancelled slots open back up for rebooking.

Fewer no-shows. More confirmed visits. More predictable revenue.

5. Learns Your Business Inside & Out

NEX is trained on your website, services, pricing, policies, FAQs, and everything unique about your business. It sounds like a trained human employee — but faster and more accurate.

  • Trains instantly from your website + business form inputs.
  • Understands services, pricing, hours, and policies.
  • Answers complex questions without hesitation.

No constant retraining. No “let me check.” Just consistent, perfect information every time.

6. Learns Faster Than Any Employee — Never Forgets

Every interaction teaches NEX more about how your customers talk and what your business needs. It remembers everything permanently.

  • Remembers returning customers & past visits.
  • Stores all call & SMS history inside your CRM.
  • Updates instantly when your business changes.

Your receptionist gets better every day — and never loses knowledge.

How to Set Up Your Receptionist

Most businesses are fully set up and taking calls with NEX in under 10 minutes. No phone provider changes. No complicated wiring. Just follow the steps below.

Step 1

Create Your Account

Subscribe and instantly get access to your NEX onboarding portal, where you’ll enter basic business information.

  • Choose your plan.
  • Secure your login.
  • Receive your NEX receptionist number.
Step 2

Connect Your Calendar & Business Info

Give NEX the information it needs to talk like a trained front desk employee.

  • Connect your calendar on the app.
  • Enter services, pricing, hours, location, and FAQs.
  • Provide your website for instant data training.
Step 3

Turn On Call Forwarding

Forward calls from your existing business number to your NEX number. Start with missed calls only or after-hours only if you prefer.

  • Choose full, after-hours, or missed-call forwarding.
  • Make test calls to hear your receptionist in action.
  • Adjust greeting or responses anytime.
Step 4

Enable Reminders & Automations

Turn on SMS reminders and advanced automations to reduce no-shows and improve customer experience.

  • Enable appointment confirmations + reminders.
  • Set routing rules for VIP customers or emergencies.
  • Add or update FAQs anytime with one click.
